\section{Why Negative Contributions Are Valid}
% ============================================================

\begin{theorem}
Individual pair contributions $S_{\ell_1,\ell_2}$ are NOT constrained to be positive.
\end{theorem}

The optimization exploits \textbf{destructive interference}: by choosing $P_2$ and $P_3$ polynomials that create negative cross-products on portions of $[0,1]$, the pairs (1,3) and (2,3) flip sign, reducing $S_{12}$ and thereby reducing $c$.

Since $\kappa = 1 - \log(c)/R$, reducing $c$ directly improves $\kappa$.
